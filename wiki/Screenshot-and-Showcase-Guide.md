# Screenshot and Showcase Guide

No screenshots ship with 1.0.0 until the owner captures genuine j3w1zsh sessions and visually sanitizes every pixel. Do not relabel historical images or use fabricated mockups as product evidence.

## Capture checklist

- Use the lowercase command, profile, scheme, paths, and session names.
- Capture native output from the actual supported platform.
- Check usernames, hostnames, IPs, private paths, repository names, branches, notifications, QR codes, fingerprints, tokens, prompts, histories, and editor buffers.
- Inspect cropped and full-resolution pixels; text-only scans are insufficient.
- Confirm no other application window, browser tab, terminal scrollback, or notification exposes private data.
- Preserve original private captures outside the public repository.
- Export only the deliberately sanitized derivative.

Recommended future views are the help/plan output, responsive Neovim `Space ?` sheet, and safe multi-client `tma` picker. Keep the README free of broken placeholders while captures are pending.
